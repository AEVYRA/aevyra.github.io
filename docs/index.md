# Welcome to Aevyra Docs

This repository contains foundational papers, documentation, and conceptual essays regarding the Aevyra architecture, temporal models, and the architecture of a portable synthetic subject.

## Papers (September 2026 revision cycle)

Reading order of the six-paper cycle:

1. [The Tensor of Time: Causal Order, Local Clocks, and Temporal Transport](tensor-of-time.md) — local duration and comparison across heterogeneous observers, without a privileged global clock.
2. [The Field of Will: Recomputable Evaluation and Authored Continuation](field-of-will.md) — authorship as a computable, recomputable structure over a self-graph.
3. [The Crystal of Will: Weighted Geometry, Refraction, and Semantic Lineage](crystal-of-will.md) — a weighted spectral model of how inherited evaluative structure refracts a present field.
4. [The Ledger of Will: Causal Evidence, Controlled Sufficiency, and Sovereign Memory](ledger-of-will.md) — memory as a faithful, valenced, sovereign structure with explicit limits.
5. [The Resonance of Will: Endogenous Appraisal, Mood, and Substrate Pull](resonance-of-will.md) — affect as the substrate's voice; appraisal, mood, and endogenous pull.
6. [The Flow of Will: Restricted Dynamical Hypotheses and Tests of Coupling](flow-of-will.md) — one generator, five projections, and decidable coupling tests.
