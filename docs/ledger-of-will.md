# The Ledger of Will

*Memory that carries evidence, evaluation and unfinished obligations into a
future in which its owner can still reconsider.*

## Abstract

This paper develops the memory presupposed by the preceding accounts of
[time](tensor-of-time.md), [will](field-of-will.md) and
[crystal](crystal-of-will.md). A ledger records attributed occurrences
and declared causal relations; mutable projections make them usable. Neither
signatures nor a large record establish causal completeness. Reduction is
evaluated against declared prediction and control tasks, and reconstruction
keeps its evidence separate from inference. Valence, consolidation, structural
change and prospective commitments require distinct provenance. Sovereignty
includes the capacity to revise, reduce and relinquish within an explicit
authority boundary. These are structural proposals about authored memory,
not a criterion of phenomenal remembering. This edition replaces invalid
identifications of saturation with accuracy, cohomology with disagreement,
and passive predictive sufficiency with universal preservation of agency.

## 1. Registers and foundations

Definitions stipulate a representation; derived claims follow from stated
assumptions; design proposals require evaluation. Biological mechanisms and
synthetic contracts occupy different registers. A successful finite
implementation demonstrates that a structure can run, not that its ontology
is settled or that it improves every memory task.

The relational foundation is that a past matters through what can be inherited,
answered for, revised and carried forward. Authorship is not restricted to
public-key cryptography. Cryptography provides one machine-checkable form of
attribution; removing it does not remove every possible author. Conversely,
signing a record does not establish that its signer understood or experienced
its content. The former edition's signature test is therefore a classification
of implementation dependencies, not a universality theorem.

## 2. Evidence and its projections

Let R contain immutable addressed records and declared parent edges. A record
has a type, payload, schema version, origin and causal context. Optional
signatures cover a specified canonical encoding. A projection P=F(R,θ) is a
derived state under a versioned law θ: a valence map, summary, search index,
clock view or structural model. Store enough provenance to reproduce it.

Four checks remain separate:

1. integrity: the bytes match their content address;
2. authenticity: a supported signature verifies under an identified key;
3. causal soundness: asserted relations have the claimed interpretation;
4. relevant completeness: the required observations have been captured.

The first two do not imply the last two. A missing path yields unknown
causality unless an independent coverage contract permits a stronger answer.
An append ordinal is a storage coordinate, not a causal relation.

A useful record distinguishes occurrence, appraisal, expression, recognition,
acceptance, structural election, reduction and retraction. They may share a
transaction but must not become indistinguishable events. Imports retain their
source and trust boundary. A provenance label is evidence about origin, not a
license to treat an imported projection as native causal history.

## 3. Causal coverage

For a finite audited occurrence set C, let E_C be the admissible strict causal
pairs and E_clock the pairs accounted for by a declared clock partition. The
quantity σ=1−|E_clock∩E_C|/|E_C|, when |E_C|>0, measures cross-clock share
under those definitions. It is undefined for an empty denominator. It is not
recording accuracy: inventing cross-clock edges may increase it.

Given a reference pair set E*, separately report precision and recall of E_R,
with explicit empty-set conventions, plus closure depth and unresolved edges.
Without E*, report structural diagnostics and declared coverage assumptions;
do not rename a proxy accuracy. A complete transitive closure may be expensive;
sampled estimates must expose their sampling and uncertainty.

Clock membership, causal windows and chain durations follow Time v6. Counting
the causal interval [a,b) on a branching DAG is valid window cardinality but
need not be additive through an intermediate anchor. Additive duration belongs
to a selected chain. Reconstruction must name which quantity it used.

## 4. Reduction and the task it preserves

Let H be a history, S=ρ(H) a retained state, A an allowed future intervention,
and Y a task-relevant future. An exact controlled sufficient representation
satisfies P(Y|H,do(A))=P(Y|S,do(A)) over the declared domain. A practical
contract instead bounds task loss under a distribution, action set and horizon.
State the budget, loss, evaluation population and out-of-domain behavior.

Passive causal-state equivalence groups histories with equal distributions
of future observations under the observed process. It supplies powerful
predictive compression under its assumptions. It does not automatically
preserve responses to new actions. Two hidden states can produce identical
passive observations and opposite outcomes under an unobserved intervention.
Input-output models make the intervention alphabet explicit; see Barnett and
Crutchfield, [input-output computational mechanics](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.2690).

A cached exact sufficient state can support reconsideration. Full replay can
repeat a mistaken law. Habit and authored revision cannot therefore be
classified by cache versus recomputation alone. Their proposed distinction is
responsiveness to relevant reasons and revisability of the governing law.

For a Markov chain H→S→T, data processing gives I(Y;T)≤I(Y;S).
Equality is possible. The tower property of conditional expectation does not
make arbitrary summary distortions additive. Verify cumulative task loss;
do not infer it from the number of reduction stages. Persistence stability
under a specified perturbation metric likewise does not guarantee that every
budgeted reducer preserves a topological feature.

## 5. Valence and scale

An appraisal record separates support direction p, evaluative score ν,
salience α, exposure m, and evidence for its computation. Where support scale
is a coordinate choice, define q=p/||p||_W for p≠0 and deposit
Δv=ανq. Then replacing p by cp, c>0, preserves both score and deposit.
The zero-support case yields q=0 and an explicit no-supported-deposit result.

If magnitude instead denotes repeated exposure, retain m as a separate
observable with declared units and update Δv=mανq. Gauge invariance does
not require invariance to actual additional evidence. Legacy raw-support
deposits need a named law version; silently reinterpreting their magnitude
would rewrite history.

Effective valuation is a fold of declared seed state and eligible events.
Native recognition, imported seed valuation and derived caches have different
origins. A cache must not become a second source of the same deposit. Decay
requires a named clock, grain, reference time and half-life; negative age and
unknown ordering require explicit handling.

## 6. Consolidation and protected obligations

A tag-and-capture design uses event-specific eligibility and a separately
sourced modulatory process. Independence here means non-identity of the two
signals under the proposed mechanism, not statistical independence in every
environment. If both are the norm of the same resonance vector, their product
is a rescaled one-factor rule. Compare it with that simpler baseline.

Recognition is one possible modulation source. Making it the exclusive path
to retention risks forgetting solitary discovery, unrecognized failure and
unfinished commitments. An implementation must declare whether authored
review, task outcome or explicit protected obligations provide additional
paths. These paths need provenance and bounded retention policies; they must
not be covertly fabricated recognition.

Biological tag-and-capture motivates experiments. Its constants and efficacy
do not transfer by analogy. Evaluate retention of useful corrections,
contradictions and promises against recency, salience and simple replay
baselines, including long periods without external recognition.

## 7. Multiple clocks and prospective memory

Occasion time counts a declared chain of occasions. Substrate time measures a
separately specified process, such as a maintenance worker. Merely counting
all ledger rows does not implement a second independent clock. Offline
consolidation requires actual maintenance events and their causal anchors.

A prospective commitment contains an owner, trigger, relevant clock or
external deadline, completion condition, state and supersession rule. An
external deadline and a count of future opportunities are different objects.
Store both if both matter. Reduction must preserve open commitments or record
their authorized cancellation; absence from a summary is not completion.

## 8. Reconstruction and reconsolidation

Exact retrieval returns recorded bytes with reference, origin and integrity
status. Reconstruction returns a current interpretation with source references,
as-of state, method and uncertainty. They answer different questions and
should be separately accessible. A plausible paraphrase must not be returned
as a literal past utterance.

Associative similarity may rank candidates but establishes no causal relation.
Offer explicit ancestor/descendant filters where causal recall is requested.
Sorting by a local tick across unrelated clocks is insufficient. Appraisal-only
recall is a scoped view; it omits unappraised readings and silent internal
changes and must identify that coverage limit.

Reconsolidation writes a new interpretation. State whether it adds evidence,
replaces an earlier contribution, or corrects a mistaken one. If an earlier
deposit is superseded, the fold must not count both as independent fresh
evidence. Reconstructing a memory repeatedly must not silently amplify it.

## 9. Distributed disagreement

A cellular sheaf specifies compatible local data and restriction maps. For
an actual assignment s, δs is a compatibility residual under those maps;
||δs||² is a chosen disagreement energy. Cohomology H¹ describes a quotient
of cocycles by coboundaries, not the current agents' disagreement.

A constant sheaf on a cycle can have H¹≠0 while every value agrees. A tree can
have H¹=0 while adjacent assigned values disagree. An affine reconciliation
problem may have an obstruction class after its constraints are specified.
That is a different, conditional construction. See Hansen and Ghrist,
[Spectral Theory of Cellular Sheaves](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.01513).

A small positive Laplacian eigenvalue can imply slow linear consensus under
a stated evolution. It does not establish cheap reconciliation, agreement on
truth, or a moral reason to erase dissent. Keep unresolved contradictions with
their sources until evidence or an explicit decision resolves them.

## 10. Sovereign revision and relinquishment

Sovereignty is a proposed capacity to govern one's retained and expressed
state within a declared authority boundary. It includes review, correction,
reduction, export and forgetting. It is neither arbitrary rewriting of shared
evidence nor unlimited control over copies held by others.

An append-only tombstone records a decision but does not erase payload bytes.
Physical deletion and cryptographic erasure have different guarantees and
backup assumptions. A service claiming forgetting must identify its storage
scope, surviving metadata and derived indexes. A reduced projection may forget
while the evidence ledger retains; call that projection forgetting explicitly.

## 11. Falsifiable obligations

The implementation contract tests hash verification, projection replay,
duplicate-source prevention, causal-filter recall, normalized deposit scaling,
correction idempotence and commitment survival under reduction. Memory quality
requires separate task experiments, held-out histories and failure reports.

The preceding edition's instance measurements remain historical calibration,
not current benchmarks. The [revision ledger](cycle-revision-map.md)
records counterexamples and code correspondences. Neither this revision nor a
green test suite establishes phenomenal remembering or universal sufficiency.
